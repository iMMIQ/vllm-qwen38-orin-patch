最新GEMM和WY在已有算子验证中逐位一致；不代表完整W8A8与W4A16一致。
融合对平均NLL影响小，但归约舍入会影响后续量化边界，可能放大为明显的token排名变化。
这组正文数据中，W8-only与完整优化的top1分歧约4.3%，因此不能宣称整套融合无损。
原W4权重=(q4-zero_group)*scale_group；临时W8=round(W4/scale_channel)*scale_channel。
group128有各自scale，改用一个输出channel scale会再次舍入；8bit临时视图不会还原原BF16权重。
W8 per-channel scale跨整个K维，A8 scale跨每个token的所有channel；outlier可能放大其它值的量化步长。
GDN state与KV仍使用FP32/FP16，但保存由量化投影算出的状态，后续原生decode仍会继承prefill差异。

边界：
This is a local diagnostic corpus, not a standard perplexity benchmark or task accuracy score.
2K texts are prefixes of the corresponding 8K texts; aggregate counts overlapping tokens and samples are correlated.
Chinese chat sample includes repeated user context. Its user-input NLL is excluded from aggregate PPL; it is not an assistant-answer quality metric.
English/Italian/code sources have limited diversity.
Top1 disagreement is under identical teacher-forced prefixes, not an error rate on an answer benchmark.
Coarse KL uses shared returned top-5/observed-token IDs plus OTHER; it is a lower bound on full-vocabulary KL.
Baseline is already AWQ Q4. This does not measure AWQ loss relative to original BF16 weights.
Reference answers use older W4A16-generated fragments as fixed targets, not human correctness labels. They are teacher-forced as prefill, and do not simulate subsequent single-token Q4 decode.
No executable code-agent, long output or retrieval accuracy evaluation.
