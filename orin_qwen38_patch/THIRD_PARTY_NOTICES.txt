This distribution adds runtime hooks and model-specific kernels to a separately
installed vLLM environment. It is not an official vLLM release.

vLLM: Apache-2.0, Copyright contributors to the vLLM project.
https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/tree/v0.20.0
The patch does not bundle or modify vLLM source files; hooks call its interfaces.

TileLang: MIT, Copyright (c) Tile-AI.
https://github.com/tile-ai/tilelang
The SM87 cubins contain compiled TileLang tl_templates code.
See LICENSES/TileLang-MIT.txt. Corresponding TileLang source and the build script
are included. The compiler, CUDA toolkit, and generated intermediate CUDA files
are not bundled.

The patch was developed with AI assistance. Model weights and dependency
packages are not distributed here; their licenses remain separate.
